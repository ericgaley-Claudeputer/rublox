🛗 Wobblevator

Ride Bingle the Bellhop’s wobbly lift between surprise floors. Clear the mini-event, hop back in, and see how far your streak can go. If a floor gets too wobbly, everyone is okay — you’re back in the lift with a fresh streak.

Keyboard: WASD move · Space jump · E act · Esc closes this help.

Mobile: Move with the stick, jump with the button, and tap the yellow action button.

Xbox: Left stick move · Ⓐ jump · Ⓧ act · Ⓑ or Esc close popups.

In single-player, quirky passengers may ride along. In multiplayer, only real friends share the lift.
