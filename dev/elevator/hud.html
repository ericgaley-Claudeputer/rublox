🛗 Wobblevator

Ride Bingle the Bellhop’s wobbly lift between surprise floors. Clear the mini-event, hop back in, and see how far your streak can go. If a floor gets too wobbly, everyone is okay — you’re back in the lift with a fresh streak.

Open Floors to see your collection. Floors don’t repeat until you’ve seen most of them. Snow Festival days unlock cozy holiday stops, and a rare secret floor is hiding in the shaft.

Keyboard: WASD move · Space jump · E act · I floors · H help · Esc closes popups.

Mobile: Move with the stick, jump with the button, and tap the yellow action button or Floors.

Xbox: Left stick move · Ⓐ jump · Ⓧ act · Ⓨ floors · Ⓑ or Esc close popups.

In single-player, quirky passengers may ride along. In multiplayer, only real friends share the lift — and some floors let friends light two pads together.
