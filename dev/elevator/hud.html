🛗 Wobblevator

Hang out in the lobby, check the streak board, and browse the shop. Then walk into the big glowing elevator doors — they are right in front of you, and the hint says Step in to ride! Once you are inside, the doors-closing countdown starts on its own. You can also press the on-screen Ride button, E, or Xbox Ⓧ. There is room for a whole group of friends (up to about 10). If you miss the doors, you wait in the cab and catch the next stop. Everyone rides to the same floor.

Clear the mini-event to grow your coin streak bonus. Shop floors show up every 15 clears, and a special floor every 25. If a floor gets too wobbly, everyone is okay — you hop back in and the streak resets. Open Floors for your collection. Find ride tickets on some floors and spend one to pick the next stop. During the ride, listen for original elevator muzak, a ticking floor dial, and the occasional harmless wobble, confetti, or lucky coin drip.

Quirky passengers ride along in solo and in the shared multiplayer cab. They chat, react to floors, hop on at their favorite stops, and sometimes hop off at a shop. Give a Ribbon Wrap to a passenger with E / Ⓧ. The host decides who boards or leaves and what they say, and everyone sees the same riders and the same speech bubbles. Bingle still talks over the intercom too. Passengers are friendly riders — they do not block the way, take a real-player slot, or change the score.

Check Today for a daily challenge (coins or a ticket, no extra purchase needed). Open Badges for your collection. There is a tiny secret in the lobby if you look behind the streak board.

Stranger floors can feel a little hush-y. Turn on Less scary anytime for a milder mood. Still no gore, and everyone is okay.

Keyboard: WASD move · Space jump · E act / shop · I floors · H help · Esc closes popups.

Mobile: Move with the stick, jump with the button, and tap Ride, Shop, Floors, Badges, Today, or Less scary.

Xbox: Left stick move · Ⓐ jump · Ⓧ act / shop · Ⓨ floors · Ⓑ or Esc close popups.
